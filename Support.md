# Kinetic Support

Need help with Kinetic, found a bug, or have a feature suggestion? [Email Kinetic Support](mailto:kinetichealthsupport@gmail.com).

If you’re reporting a problem, please include your **iPhone model**, **iOS version**, **Kinetic version**, and a short description of what happened. If you attach a screenshot, crop out any personal health information.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why aren’t my steps showing?

Open the **Health** app and check that Kinetic has permission to read your step count. If your steps were recorded on Apple Watch, check that they appear in Health on your iPhone, then reopen Kinetic.

### Why does my walking estimate show zero?

Kinetic uses walking speed and stride length to estimate how far and how long you’ll need to walk. If Apple Health doesn’t have those measurements, you can enter your own values under **Walking Speed Configuration** and **Stride Length Configuration** in Kinetic’s **Settings**.

### How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

In Kinetic, go to **Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.
