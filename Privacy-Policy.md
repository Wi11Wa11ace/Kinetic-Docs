# Kinetic Privacy Policy

**Last updated 8 November 2026**

This policy explains how Kinetic: Step Tracker, an iPhone app developed by Will Wallace in Australia, handles information. Kinetic does not require you to create a Kinetic account.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Kinetic reads your **step count, walking speed and walking step length** from Apple Health through HealthKit. Kinetic uses these measurements to show your daily progress, weekly step history and goal streak, and to estimate the distance and walking time remaining to reach your step goal.

If you choose to log steps manually and give Kinetic write permission, Kinetic saves the step count and the time interval you confirm to Apple Health. Apple Health manages those entries after they are saved.

Kinetic processes Apple Health data on your device. Kinetic does not send that data to the developer, Google AdMob or other advertising services, and does not use it to target ads.

## On-device storage, widgets and notifications

Kinetic stores your preferences and cached data on your device, including step goals, pace and stride settings, recent calculations, streak information, widget display data and whether Remove Ads is active. The app shares the data needed by its widgets through Apple's on-device App Group storage.

If you enable notifications, Kinetic schedules progress updates and goal alerts locally on your device. Your notification and widget settings determine what may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

## Advertising

Kinetic uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner ads. The SDK is initialised when Kinetic starts. Google may receive information from the SDK, such as your IP address, device or app identifiers where available, ad impressions and interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics. An IP address may be used to infer a general location. Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, analyse performance and diagnose problems. Google may process this information outside Australia. Its handling of that information is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The optional Remove Ads purchase hides Kinetic's banner ads.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. Kinetic uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether Remove Ads has been purchased or restored and stores that status on your device. Kinetic does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to you and investigate the issue. Please leave out health records and crop personal health information from screenshots. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve and follow up on your request or to meet applicable obligations. You can contact support to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your support information, or to raise a privacy concern.

## Your choices and deletion

- **Apple Health permissions.** You can change Kinetic's read and write permissions in the Health app. Revoking access stops future access and does not delete entries already saved to Apple Health or data already cached by Kinetic.
- **Manual step entries.** You can review and delete steps saved by Kinetic in the Health app.
- **Notifications and widgets.** You can disable Kinetic notifications in iPhone Settings and remove its widgets from the Home Screen or Lock Screen.
- **Local app data.** Delete Kinetic from your device to remove its local app data. Steps already saved to Apple Health and purchases recorded by Apple are managed separately.
- **Third-party data.** For information held by Google or Apple, use the privacy controls and request options in their policies linked above.
